The process

Six steps from first call to activation

This is the exact flow our agents follow, from the official ANS Technocom process guide. Nothing to memorise — the telecalling app prompts you at every step.

01

Make calls & dispose correctly

Call customers using the telecalling app. After every call, select the correct disposition — No Answer, Switched Off, Not Interested, Lead, etc. — so the pipeline stays clean and follow-ups land on the right people.

02

Capture interested customers

For customers who show interest, either fill the online application on their behalf, or send them the application link to complete themselves.

03

Soft approval → push for VKYC

If the customer is soft-approved based on CIBIL score, follow up to get them through Video KYC quickly.

Most time-sensitive step
04

Task marked complete

Once VKYC is done, your part for that customer is complete. No further action needed at this stage.

05

Bank processing & approval

The bank independently evaluates the application on CIBIL score, location, and its own policies, then approves or declines.

06

Encourage card activation

Once approved, follow up so the customer activates the card — typically a small spend of around Rs. 100. Activation protects the whole pipeline and keeps the customer's card in good standing.
